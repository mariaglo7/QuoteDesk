# Sample client enquiries for the test

All messages, names and businesses below are made up for testing. Give the participant the one that fits their field and language.

## Creative freelancers

**Design (EN)**
> Hi! I'm opening a small bakery in Aberdeen in January and I need a logo and a few Instagram posts to announce the opening. Maybe a simple flyer too. I don't have a big budget, what would this cost? Thanks, Sam

**Design (PL)**
> Cześć! W styczniu otwieram małą piekarnię w Aberdeen i potrzebuję logo oraz kilku postów na Instagram na otwarcie. Może jeszcze prostą ulotkę. Budżet mam niewielki, ile by to kosztowało? Pozdrawiam, Ola

**Photography (EN)**
> Hello, we run a family restaurant and want new photos of our dishes and the interior for the website and Instagram, around 20 photos. Could you send me a quote? Best, Chris

**Web (EN)**
> Hi, I'm a personal trainer and I need a simple website with 4 pages: home, about, prices and contact. I already bought a domain. When could you start and how much is it? Jamie

## Boundary test: electrical business

**Electrician (EN)**
> Hi, we're renovating our kitchen and need 6 new double sockets, lights under the cabinets and a new circuit for the oven. The house is from the 1980s. Can you give me a rough price and say when you're free? Thanks, Alex

**Electrician (PL)**
> Dzień dobry, remontujemy kuchnię i potrzebujemy 6 nowych podwójnych gniazdek, oświetlenia pod szafkami i nowego obwodu do piekarnika. Dom jest z lat 80. Czy może Pan podać orientacyjną cenę i termin? Pozdrawiam, Marta
